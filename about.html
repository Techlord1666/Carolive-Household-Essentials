Terms of Service

1. Service Acceptance: By accessing and purchasing from Carolive Store, you agree to comply with our commercial terms. We provide high-quality home essentials for the modern Nigerian household.

2. Product Representation: We strive to display high-resolution images. However, slight variations in color may occur due to device settings. We guarantee that the physical build matches our reliable standards.

3. Pricing & Payments: Prices are quoted in Nigerian Naira (₦). For nationwide delivery outside Lagos, full payment must be verified before we dispatch goods. Order confirmation happens via our official WhatsApp sales channel.

4. Order Modifications: Once an order is dispatched to our logistics partners, it cannot be canceled. Modifications must be done immediately after payment and before fulfillment starts.

Returns & Refunds

1. Eligibility Window: We offer a 48-hour return window from the time of delivery if the item received is damaged or incorrect. Items must be in their original premium packaging, unused, and unwashed.

2. The Process: If you receive a faulty item, please take a photo and send it to our WhatsApp (+234 703 624 7597) immediately. We will arrange a pickup and provide an exchange for the correct item.

3. Refunds: We primarily offer exchanges. If an item is out of stock and an exchange isn't possible, we process bank refunds within 7 working days of receiving the returned goods.

4. Hygiene: For hygiene reasons, bedding and towels that have been removed from packaging are non-returnable unless there is a manufacturing defect.
